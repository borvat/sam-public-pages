Privacy Policy

1. Operator and scope

SAM is a private executive operations platform operated by Surooh Holding Group B.V. This draft explains its intended handling of information accessed from authorized Google accounts.

2. Data accessed

With account-owner authorization, SAM may access Gmail messages and metadata via gmail.readonly and Google Drive file/folder metadata via drive.metadata.readonly. The latter permission does not grant access to document contents.

3. Purposes

Authorized business correspondence review, file discovery, operational coordination, and internal reporting.

4. Google API data and limited use

SAM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is intended to adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

5. Service providers and storage

SAM uses connected application infrastructure. Its exact processors, storage locations, access controls, and data flows must be confirmed in an operational review before this draft becomes an official policy.

6. Security

Access is intended to be limited to authorized operations. Implemented access controls, safeguards and incident procedures are subject to verification before official publication.

7. Retention and deletion

Actual retention periods, logs, backups, and deletion procedures require verification. Requests about access or deletion can be sent to the operator at the contact email below.

8. Requests and contact

Contact ceo@suroohholdings.com for privacy requests. Applicable privacy rights depend on the relevant laws and circumstances.

9. Updates

The operator may update this draft to reflect verified implementation and applicable requirements.
