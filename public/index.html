SAM — Executive Operations Platform

SAM is a private business operations platform operated by Surooh Holding Group B.V. for use by its authorized owner. It assists with reviewing information, coordinating operational workflows, and preparing reports under human oversight.

Google services used

  • Gmail: read-only access to authorized email messages and associated information (gmail.readonly).
  • Google Drive: read-only access to file and folder metadata, not document contents (drive.metadata.readonly).

Limited access

The application is not offered for public registration. It does not request Google scopes to send emails or modify Google Drive files. Information received through Google APIs is not sold or used for advertising.
